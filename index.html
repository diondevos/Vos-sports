The exercise first. Its name on a line, the dates under it. The weight goes in brackets and the reps follow, separated by dashes: (45kg) 14-10-7 is three sets at 45 kg, of 14, 10 and 7 reps. A new weight mid-line changes it from there on — (16kg) 19 (20kg) 8-6 is 16 kg × 19, then 20 kg × 8 and 20 kg × 6. Reps with no weight are read as bodyweight and shown at 0 kg. Write N.V.T. on a date the exercise was not done and it is reported, never guessed.

Or the date first, with the exercises under it — weight before the ×, reps after, and 72.5 x 5 x 3 means three sets of five. A line Fat mass 14.5% under a date records a measurement. A note in brackets after a set — 100 x 5 (felt heavy) — sticks to that set; a line of words with no numbers under a date is kept as the session's note. Dates can be 12-09-26, 07-09-2026, 2026-09-07 or 7 Sep 2026 — day before month, Dutch month names too.
