A date on its own line starts a session; the lines under it are exercises. Weight comes first, reps after the ×. 72.5 x 5 x 3 means three sets of five. A line Fat mass 14.5% under a date records a measurement. A note in brackets after a set — 100 x 5 (felt heavy) — sticks to that set; a line of words with no numbers under a date is kept as the session's note. Dates can be 7 Sep 2026, 07-09-2026 or 2026-09-07 — day before month. Dutch month names work too.
